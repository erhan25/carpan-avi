Privacy Policy

Factor Hunt · Last updated: 2026-09-27

Summary

Factor Hunt ("the game") is made by Matoyun. The game does not need an account, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal data on any server. Your game progress stays on your device. Optional ads are provided by Google AdMob.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, points), tokens, purchases (for example Premium), settings (sound, vibration, language, look) and the age you choose on first launch. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game removes it; "Reset progress" in Settings clears levels and stars. If device backup is turned on, Android or iOS may also copy this data to your own Google or iCloud backup so that it can be restored on a new device; we cannot access that backup.

Age screen and ad settings

On first launch the game asks your age on a neutral screen. The answer stays on your device and is used only to choose ad settings: under 13, ads are requested as child-directed and are never personalized; from 13 to 15, ads are not personalized; from 16, ads may be personalized only if you give consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), using Google's consent form.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for example to get an extra life). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and ad interactions. For child-directed and under-16 requests Google limits this processing and does not personalize ads. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Premium removes ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Premium and token packs) are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details; the store only confirms the purchase so that it can be unlocked in the game.

Diagnostics

Google Play and the App Store may share anonymous crash and performance reports with us according to their own policies. The game does not add any other analytics or tracking tools.

Children

The game is made for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no sharing with other players. Parents can contact us with any question.

Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and purchases), advertising ID (ads, as described above), vibration (feedback on a wrong tap; can be turned off in Settings) and keeping the device awake (required by the ads library).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR in the EU or KVKK in Turkey), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we keep no personal data on servers, most of it is on your device; for ad data, see Google's settings. Write to us for any request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: erhanyazici7@outlook.com
